LANTERN BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Ezra Cole fixed other people's cars for five years and stayed broke. Tonight the crews of Lantern Bay start paying him to drive, steal and fight instead. Six jobs. One city. No going back.

WASD / arrows — walk or drive  •  E — steal a car / step out
Mouse + click — aim and shoot on foot  •  R — reload
Shift — sprint  •  P — pause (auto-saves)  •  M — music on/off
Follow the yellow marker: missions pay cash, the police pay attention. Stealing cars and shooting bystanders raise your wanted level. Lose the police to cool down. Progress saves automatically.
